A blog of notes, links and essays on shipping, maintenance, dependencies and open source. Most posts run under a hundred words.

Each post is a markdown file in git, served by one Go binary, markgo, with no database. The deploy harness is MIT and made to fork. The writing is all rights reserved.

## Why it exists

A post on a platform sits behind a follower count and an algorithm deciding who reads what.

log puts short notes on a site with neither.

## What it taught

A blog needs no platform: a folder of text files and one binary are enough.

The site is the proof. The folder is `articles/` in the source.
