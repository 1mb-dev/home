drift

Always-on radio in six moods.

Mood radio. Pick a mood and it plays: Focus for deep work, Calm to wind down, Late Night for lo-fi, Energize, Groove or Storm.

Focus is instrumental ambient. Lyrics show on tracks that have them. An album plays in its own order.

It installs as an app and needs no account. A version to host yourself is public.

why it exists

Work, study and rest each want music already playing. Choosing it is one more task.

drift keeps a station for each mood and carries on from there.

what it taught

drift set out to show that a mood is enough input. Each mood shuffles for a fresh mix, and no profile decides what comes next.

Skip always works. The fourth in a row says "Drifting works best when you let go."

Source is public.
